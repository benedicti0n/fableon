# Where the Light Lands — Storyboard

**Format:** 30.000 seconds · 720 frames · 24 fps · 1920×1080 · silent  
**Rendering:** Fableon Canvas 2D, local Chromium frame rendering, local FFmpeg H.264 encode  
**Character:** Nine-part Little Bird rig and Phase 2.7 candidate parts  
**Environment:** Conservative two-plane parallax derived from the Phase 2.8 painted start frame  
**Paid API usage:** Zero

## Logline

A small woodland bird crosses a sunlit forest, finds a moss-covered branch in a pool of
morning light, lands carefully, and settles into a quiet moment.

## Visual direction

- Warm morning forest with golden light from the upper right.
- Soft watercolor/gouache surface inherited from the existing painted plate.
- Restrained camera motion: a reveal, a gentle follow, a settle, then a slow push-in.
- Calm storybook pacing. No camera cuts inside a shot; shot changes use an explicit
  crossfade or fade.
- No narration, music, or sound effects in this phase. The exported film is intentionally
  silent.

## Story beats

1. **0.0–1.0 s:** black gives way to the painted forest; the opening title fades in.
2. **1.0–6.0 s:** the camera reveals the branch and sunlight; ambient pollen and leaf sway
   establish a living but calm forest.
3. **6.0–15.0 s:** the bird enters from the left and crosses the frame. Wing beats and tail
   follow-through carry the flight rather than a simple static translation.
4. **15.0–19.6 s:** the bird descends toward the branch, spreads its wings to brake, and
   extends its legs. The lower foot reaches the branch at global frame 470.
5. **19.6–22.0 s:** the bird compresses into the landing, folds its wings, and settles.
6. **22.0–30.0 s:** the bird rests. A slow camera push-in, gentle idle motion, ending title,
   and final fade complete the story.

## Titles

- Ending: **Sometimes, the smallest journeys lead us home.** (frames 600–688, fade in/out
  18 frames).

## What this storyboard is not

The environment is not a newly painted multilayer forest. It is a conservative parallax
demonstration built from one existing painted plate and a softened distant copy. The bird
parts are the Phase 2.7 candidate assets, not approved production artwork. The film is a
complete local animation pipeline demonstration, not a claim of production-ready
illustration quality.
