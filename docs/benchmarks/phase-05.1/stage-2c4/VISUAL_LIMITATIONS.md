# Stage 2C.4 — Visual Limitations

These are technical renders, not approved production artwork.

## What is confirmed

- The OpenAI upper arm and forearm preserve the blue sleeve, floral motif, cuff, skin tone and dark outline.
- The Gemini paid arm no longer has green-dominant opaque pixels after Stage 2C.4 extraction. Its geometric ochre/blue design differs from the OpenAI floral design.
- Both paid arm sets attach to the existing torso and rotate through neutral, reach, elbow −45° and elbow −90°.
- Both 24-frame clips use the paid arm and forearm and remain visually animated for the full second.

## What is not confirmed

### Arm seams

The shoulder seam is acceptable in the neutral frame, but the paid upper arm is narrower than the stage 2B sleeve and the fit is not artistically approved. The elbow stress frames use the production debug skeleton overlay; they are diagnostics, not final presentation frames.

### Hands

The paid forearm includes a hand. The old hand settings are therefore hidden in the technical pack. The hand is not independently articulated in this phase.

### Legs

The paid thigh and shin are loaded and registered, but the one-second clip is arm-led. A separate leg-only diagnostic was not produced. The near dhoti and near foot are hidden because the paid thigh/shin already contain those regions. This is a technical integration, not a leg-quality claim.

### Gemini identity

The Gemini torso, face and palette are preserved, but the paid arm's geometric pattern does not read as the same decorative language as the OpenAI floral sleeve. The Gemini character is intentionally not substituted with OpenAI art.

### Mask editor

The persistent mask workflow exists as a script and stores revisions, but the interactive brush/erase/undo mask editor required by the broader Stage 2C plan remains unimplemented. No mask is marked artistically approved.

### Neutral reconstruction metrics

No matched old-vs-new neutral reconstruction IoU or landmark displacement measurement was produced in Stage 2C.4. The arm frames are not being used to claim a numerical quality improvement.

### Approval

`reviewStatus` for every generated candidate remains `technical-test-only`. No artwork is production-approved.
